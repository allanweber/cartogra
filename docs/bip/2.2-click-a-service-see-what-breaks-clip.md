**Pre-record checklist**
- Seed a graph where `payments-api` has a visible 3-hop fan-out and at least two owning teams.
- Include one cycle so the badge shows.
- Do not include an orphan in the shot unless it's the point — it triggers the SPOF/orphan styling and muddies the message.
- Re-record if the on-graph highlight or panel layout changes; clip goes stale fast.

- **Caption (150–200 words):** Most service catalogs show you a graph and stop. Cartogra answers the question you actually ask during an incident: if this service goes down, what breaks? Click a node and everything outside its blast radius fades. The side panel groups affected services by distance — direct dependents first, then second and third hop — with the owning team beside each, so you know who to page. Under the hood it's a single recursive query against a materialized view, with depth and size caps that tell you when the answer was truncated rather than quietly cutting it off.
